Who Came to Australia?

A visual story of Australia’s migration waves: where migrants came from, which states they moved to, who they were, and why they came.

Section 1

Australia’s Migration Wave

Australia’s migration story has shifted dramatically over the past two decades. This section gives an overview of total migration and how it differs by state.

Net overseas migration by state

A choropleth map showing which states received the largest net migration.

Net migration trend over 20 years

A line chart showing long-term migration change, including the COVID drop and rebound.

Permanent, temporary and citizen movement

A stacked area chart showing how the structure of migration changed over time.

Section 2

Where Did Migrants Come From?

Migration to Australia is not evenly distributed across regions and countries. This section compares origin countries and broader world regions.

Top 10 birth countries

Grouped bars compare the leading countries in 2018–19 and 2024–25.

Migration by world region

A multi-line chart compares regional recovery after the pandemic.

Fastest-growing migration regions

A custom lollipop chart ranks regional growth from 2018–19 to 2023–24.

Section 3

Who Were the Migrants?

Age and gender reveal whether migration is mainly driven by students, workers, families, or older arrivals.

Age and gender profile

A butterfly bar chart shows the age structure of arrivals.

How the age profile changed

A butterfly chart compares age distribution between 2018–19 and 2024–25.

Section 4

Why Did They Come?

Visa categories help explain the purpose of migration, including skilled, family, student and temporary work pathways.

Permanent visa program composition

Stacked bars compare family, skilled and child visa programs.

Temporary visa net flow heatmap
